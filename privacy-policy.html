Corporate Governance & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Governing Entity: Lifa Group & LifaEducare Pvt. Ltd.

1. Scope of Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and management of data by Lifa Group ("the Group"), its educational operating subsidiary LifaEducare Pvt. Ltd., and associated institutions including Aakash Institute (Hajipur Center) and St. Mary's P School (Sonpur).

2. Information Collection

We collect personal and academic data strictly necessary for institutional inquiries, academic enrollment, parent communications, and corporate correspondence:

  • Contact Identifiers: Name, professional email address, telephone/WhatsApp contact numbers.
  • Academic & Enrollment Details: Inquiries related to student grade level, test preparation batches (NEET/JEE), or school admissions.
  • Corporate Credentials: Organizational affiliation for partnership or vendor proposals.

3. Purpose of Processing

Information submitted across our communication portals is used exclusively for:

  • Responding to official corporate inquiries and scheduling consultations.
  • Processing educational inquiries for LifaEducare institutions.
  • Maintaining institutional compliance and communication records.
  • Transmitting confidential notices regarding group developments when explicitly requested.

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

Lifa Group maintains administrative and technical safeguards to preserve data confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or lease contact records or personal data to third-party commercial marketing entities under any circumstances.

5. Institutional Relationships & Third-Party Links

Our web portal references associated institutions such as Aakash Institute and St. Mary’s P School. While LifaEducare Pvt. Ltd. oversees designated center management and operations, institutional programs adhere to respective regulatory frameworks.
